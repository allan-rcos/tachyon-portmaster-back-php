Loads a quantity of a product into a container.

Follows the command shape documented on CreateProductCommand.

Carries a quantity, not a weight: what that quantity weighs follows from the product's density, and letting a caller assert it independently would allow a manifest whose arithmetic does not hold. Whether the container can take it — capacity, status, compatibility — is the table module's to decide.
